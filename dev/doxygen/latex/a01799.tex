\doxysubsection{Detailed Description}
\subsubsection*{template$<$class MI$>$\newline
struct Ikarus\+::\+Materials\+::\+Material$<$ MI $>$}
Consider a hyper elastic material with the free Helmholtz energy \[\require{cases}\psi: \begin{cases}\mathbb{R}^{3\times 3} &\rightarrow \mathbb{R} \\ \BC
&\mapsto \psi(\BC) \end{cases}.\]

Then, the value of this potential energy is return by \mbox{\hyperlink{a01799_a8d6370e3d198662f4222a0b94e2d6703}{stored\+Energy }} and its first derivative (the stresses) by \mbox{\hyperlink{a01799_a8a68e052cade23285d1a28d1e08845f5}{stresses }} and the second derivatives (the tangent moduli) by \mbox{\hyperlink{a01799_a7a97d64507fd1ce0c14e919edb8a639b}{tangent\+Moduli }}.

The passed strains can be in several formats, i.\+e., $\BC$ can be the \href{https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Finite_strain_theory\#Cauchy_strain_tensor_(right_Cauchy\%E2\%80\%93Green_deformation_tensor)}{\texttt{ right Cauchy-\/\+Green tensor}}, the \href{https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Finite_strain_theory\#Deformation_gradient_tensor}{\texttt{ deformation gradient}} $\mathbf{F}$ or linear strains. The current supported tags are given by \mbox{\hyperlink{a00496_gacef1e451de1a99845c59d7391064fb8f}{Ikarus\+::\+Strain\+Tags}}.

This assumes the existence of a complementary stored energy function \$\textbackslash{}chi(\textbackslash{}\+BS)\$, such that \$\$ \textbackslash{}partial\+\_\+\{\textbackslash{}\+BS\} \textbackslash{}chi(\textbackslash{}\+BS) \+:= \textbackslash{}\+BE\$\$. Except for linear materials, this is not just the inverse of the material tangent, but needs the inversion of the materials stored energy function. For SVK and Linear Elasticity, the inverse of \$\textbackslash{}\+BC\$ is taken. For Neo\+Hooke an analytical solution exists, and for the general hyperelastic framework (and for all materials that don\textquotesingle{}t implement the material inversion, for that a strain energy function exists) a numerical approach is used.
